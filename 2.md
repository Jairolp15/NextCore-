## Brand & Style

This design system is a high-performance evolution tailored for the next generation of digital infrastructure. It projects **technical supremacy, cinematic depth, and unwavering reliability**. Moving away from the sterile clinical feel of previous iterations, it adopts a **Corporate Modern** style infused with **Glassmorphism** and high-contrast accents to evoke the glow of fiber-optic signals within a dark-mode data center environment.

The aesthetic is defined by "The Glow"—a strategic use of amber light against deep midnight navy to simulate active data transmission. The visual language is precise and intentional, balancing the dense information requirements of infrastructure management with a premium, executive-level finish. It is designed to feel like a mission-control interface: dark, focused, and powerful.

## Colors

The palette shifts to a **strictly dark-mode foundation** to prioritize visual comfort and professional authority.

- **Primary (Midnight Navy - #0D1B2A):** The core background and structural color. It provides a deeper, more premium contrast than standard charcoal, serving as the "void" for the fiber optics to shine.
- **Secondary (Amber Gold - #E67E22):** Used for critical highlights, primary calls to action, and active states. It mimics the warmth and energy of a lit fiber strand.
- **Accent (Electric Gold - #F39C12):** A higher-vibrancy variation of the brand gold used for hover states and small-scale iconography to ensure "glow" effects remain legible.
- **Neutral/Surface:** We utilize a tiered system of navy-grays. **Surface Charcoal (#1B263B)** is used for cards and containers to create subtle separation from the base midnight background without relying on heavy borders.

## Typography

This design system continues the use of **Inter** but applies it with a more technical, condensed intent. By tightening letter-spacing on headlines, we achieve a "precision-engineered" look that feels modern and high-tech.

- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (700+) with negative tracking create an impactful hierarchy. These should be treated as structural elements within the UI.
- **Body:** Standardized at 16px for optimal legibility against dark backgrounds. We use a slightly desaturated off-white (Silver-Gray) for body text to reduce eye strain.
- **Labels:** Technical metadata and "over-lines" utilize wide-tracked uppercase styles to distinguish data points from narrative content, mimicking terminal or dashboard readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a **12-column fluid-grid** that prioritizes information density and logical grouping.

- **Grid Logic:** Content is organized into modular containers that span 4, 6, or 12 columns. We favor a "Fixed-Center" container for marketing pages (max-width 1280px) and a "Fluid-Edge" layout for technical dashboards.
- **Rhythm:** A strict 8px-based spacing system governs all component relationships. 
- **Adaptability:**
  - **Desktop:** 24px gutters provide breathing room between dense data sets.
  - **Mobile:** Gutters shrink to 16px, and margins are minimized to maximize screen real estate for technical charts and lists.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is achieved through **chromatic layering** and **light-source simulation**.

- **Tonal Layers:** We do not use traditional black shadows. Instead, we use "Lightened Surfaces." The further an element is "raised" from the base, the lighter its navy-blue background becomes. 
- **Backdrop Blurs:** High-level surfaces like modals and dropdowns utilize **Glassmorphism** (16px - 32px blur) with a 60% opacity fill of the Surface color.
- **Glow Borders:** To indicate focus or high-priority status, we use a 1px inner border in Amber Gold, accompanied by a subtle outer bloom (4px blur, 20% opacity) of the same color.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft (0.25rem)**. This provides a subtle nod to the curved geometry of the infinity-style fiber logo while maintaining a professional, "industrial-grade" rigidity.

- **Standard Elements:** Buttons, tags, and inputs use the base 4px radius.
- **Feature Cards:** Larger containers use the `rounded-lg` (8px) setting to create a distinct, modular look.
- **Icons:** System icons should follow a "monoline" style with slightly rounded terminals to match the font and border radius.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled Amber Gold with Midnight Navy text. These feature a subtle "inner-glow" on hover to simulate a lit filament.
- **Secondary:** Ghost style with a 1.5px Amber Gold border. On hover, the background fills with a 10% opacity Amber tint.
- **Utility:** Ghost style with Silver-Gray text, used for low-priority navigation.

### Data Cards
Technical containers utilize a 1px border in a slightly lighter navy than the surface. Headers within cards are separated by a subtle horizontal rule and use the `label-md` typographic style.

### Input Fields
Inputs are deep-set with a Midnight Navy background and a 1.5px border in Surface Charcoal. On focus, the border transitions to Amber Gold with a 2px "glow" bloom.

### Indicators & Chips
Status indicators (e.g., "Online," "Active") use small, circular "LED" icons that pulse. Success states use Data Blue, while warnings use the brand Amber.

### Navigation
The primary nav is a semi-transparent blur-bar at the top of the viewport. Active links are indicated by a 2px Amber Gold underline that spans only the width of the text, mirroring a high-speed data line.
